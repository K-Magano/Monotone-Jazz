MONOTONE JAZZ RECORDS

For the Love of Jazz!

Montone Jazz Records—your premier destination for jazz vinyl in South Africa. Based in the heart of Johannesburg, we specialize in curating and selling both pristine new releases and rare second-hand vinyl treasures. From timeless classics like Miles Davis and John Coltrane to exclusive collectibles and limited-edition pressings, we bring the best of jazz straight to your door with reliable, nationwide delivery. Our passion for jazz runs deep. What started as a personal love for the smooth grooves of vinyl has grown into a vibrant hub for fellow enthusiasts. Whether you're hunting for that elusive Blue Note gem, building your collection, or simply craving the warm analog sound, we've got you covered. We go beyond records too—stay in the loop with the latest jazz gigs, festival updates, and insider news from South Africa's thriving scene, handpicked to keep your playlist evolving. Discover the soul of jazz with Montone Jazz Records.
